# Casino Security Master Architecture

This document serves as the master source of truth for the physical and logic-based security of the Poopyville Casino. It details the required redstone protocols to prevent 12-year-olds from spoofing payouts, tampering with item filters, or breaking into the machines.

## 1. Calibrated Sculk Sensor Mechanics
All physical security relies on **Calibrated Sculk Sensors** tuned with Amethyst Shards and a Lectern (or comparator) to filter out ambient noise (e.g., players walking or opening Shulker boxes).
- **Frequency 12 (Signal Strength 12):** Listens ONLY for Blocks Destroyed.
- **Frequency 13 (Signal Strength 13):** Listens ONLY for Blocks Placed.
- **Euclidean Geometry:** The sensors have a 16-block true spherical radius. Because it uses Euclidean hypotenuse math (`sqrt(x^2 + y^2 + z^2) = distance`), a sensor placed 13 blocks horizontally away from a target still has roughly 9 blocks of vertical coverage.

## 2. Plinko Machine Security Protocol
Because the Plinko game is exposed to the public, it requires specialized coverage.
* **The Glass Case:** Sensors tuned to **Frequency 12 (Blocks Destroyed)** cover the entire physical encasement. *Reason: Prevents an intruder from breaking the glass to drop their own concrete powder in, or breaking the side of the case to insert a piston.*
* **The Internal Airspace & Redstone:** Sensors tuned to **Frequency 13 (Blocks Placed)** cover the interior airspace and the entirety of the Jackpot redstone wire. *Reason: If they sneak in without breaking the case, they are forced to place concrete powder to drop it, or place a redstone torch to spoof the wire. Freq 13 catches both.*
* **The Bedrock Cell (Admin Room):** A massive Bedrock room beneath the machine that hosts the Plinko logic, the centralized password box, and the Audit Ledgers.

## 3. Slot Machine Security Protocol
Each slot machine (Diamond, Enchanted Apple, etc.) has three critical failure points that must be covered:
* **The Input (Compost Bins/Filters):** Fully encased in blocks. Sensors tuned to **Frequency 12 (Blocks Destroyed)**. *Reason: Prevents players from mining the hoppers to swap out the item filters to accept dirt as tokens.*
* **The Internal Redstone (Jackpot Lines):** Insulated heavily with Wool to block outside noise. Sensors tuned to **Frequency 13 (Blocks Placed)**. *Reason: Prevents an intruder from mining into the machine and placing a redstone torch next to the output dispenser.*
* **The Jackpot Box (Payout Reserve):** Sensors tuned to **Frequency 12 (Blocks Destroyed)**. *Reason: Stops a brute-force robbery where a player mines straight into the machine to steal the house reserves.*

## 4. The Transmission Network & The 7-Tick Rule
* All sensors are locked inside **Bedrock Tubes**.
* **The 7-Tick Rule:** When a sensor trips, the signal travels through the Bedrock Tube and MUST reach the central alarm chunk within **7 game ticks**. 
* **The Observer Diode:** Once the signal enters the central chunk, it hits an Observer Line. This acts as a diode—once the observer fires, the signal is self-replicating and unstoppable. Even if the intruder snips the wire behind them, the alarm will ring.

## 5. The Audit Ledgers & Password Protocol
To prevent false bans (e.g., a child placing a Shulker box too close to an uninsulated sensor):
* The main Alarm Box is 100% encased in Bedrock, requiring a massive lever combination to disarm (mathematically brute-force proof).
* Inside the Plinko Bedrock Cell, there are physical Ledgers (Lecterns) tracking the **Super Counters**: Lifetime tokens generated, Total Plays, and Total Jackpots.
* If an alarm rings, the Admin checks the Ledgers against the physical prize pools. If the math adds up and no prize money is missing, the password box is reset without consequence.

## 6. Increment Line Routing (Logic Flow)
The Increment Line (generated by the AutoCrafter parsing 9 diamonds) routes to exactly three places:
1. The Internal All-Time Super Counter (Lifetime Tokens)
2. The Internal Source of Truth Counter (Currently available plays)
3. The External View Model (Updates the 7-segment display)
When a game is played, the Internal Source of Truth validates the balance, sends the Decrement Signal to the View Model, and drops the concrete powder.
